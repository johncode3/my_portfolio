About Me

I believe great design shouldn't just solve problems—it should look fire while doing it.

My journey started in the world of pixels and professional large-format printing, where I spent two years mastering visual communication. Now, as an IT student, I’m bridging that creative foundation with the logic of full-stack development.

My typical day? Handling graphic design and web architecture from 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM, then diving into university lectures from 6:00 PM – 9:00 PM. It’s a high-energy grind, but it allows me to build digital products that are as technically sound as they are aesthetically pleasing.

Whether I'm architecting custom WordPress / Laravel platforms, sharing student survival tips on TikTok, or documenting my coding journey, I’m focused on creating real impact through clean, thoughtful tech.
