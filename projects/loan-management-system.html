Loan Management System

Role-Based Loan Operations with Automated Amortization & Repayment Tracking

Laravel Blade MySQL Vite Tailwind CSS
View on GitHub

Overview

Cash loan teams need a reliable way to manage customers, staff, applications, approvals, disbursements, and repayments in one place. Manual tracking can make it difficult to follow each loan's status, maintain accurate customer records, and identify installments that need attention.

This Laravel application centralizes the complete loan lifecycle. Customers and staff can submit applications, authorized users can approve and disburse loans, and the system generates reducing-balance repayment schedules while dashboards track collections and overdue accounts.

Key Features

Role-Based Authentication

  • Supports admin, loan officer, cashier, and customer roles.
  • Middleware controls access to approvals, disbursements, and repayments.

Loan Application & Verification Flow

  • Customers and staff can submit applications for active customers.
  • Track the workflow from Pending to Approved to Disbursed.

Automated Repayment Schedule & Interest Calculator

  • Generate reducing-balance schedules using principal, rate, and term.
  • Adjust the final installment after rounding and show schedule totals.

Overdue Tracking & Dashboard Analytics

  • Record cash or selected payment methods against unpaid schedules.
  • Monitor overdue amounts, collections, applications, and customer totals.
